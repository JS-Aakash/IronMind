1. HYDRAULIC EQUATIONS & EFFICIENCY FORMULAS:
- Hydraulic Power Calculation (kW):
  P_hyd = (density_kg_m3 * g * Q_m3_h * H_m) / (3600 * 1000)
  where g = 9.81 m/s²

- Mechanical/Overall Pump Efficiency (%):
  Efficiency = (P_hyd / P_input_kW) * 100

2. OPERATIONAL LIMITS:
- Minimum Continuous Stable Flow (MCSF): 35% of Best Efficiency Point (BEP).
- Maximum Allowable Working Pressure (MAWP): 25.0 bar at 150°C.
- Design Service: Bottom Slurry Transfer Pump P-101A/B.
- Drive End Bearing Type: SKF Explorer Deep Groove Ball Bearing 6314 C3.
- Non-Drive End Bearing Type: SKF Duplex Angular Contact 7314 BECBM.
